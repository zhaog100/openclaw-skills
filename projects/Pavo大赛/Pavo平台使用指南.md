## 🎨 Agnes Video 2.5 使用技巧

### 提示词公式
```
[主体描述] + [动作/动态] + [环境/背景] + [风格/艺术效果] + [镜头/视角]
```

Chinese ink painting style, post-apocalyptic wasteland, cracked valley with dry earth, withered bones scattered everywhere, ancient broken flags in dust, dark smoke rising from ground, muted sepia and black tones, ink wash painting aesthetic, desolate atmosphere, dramatic lighting, cinematic composition, slow camera pan

```
Chinese ink painting style, young Taoist girl awakening from ruins, black hair with flowing ribbons, tattered Taoist robe, golden firefly light emanating from fingertips, mysterious ancient temple background, ethereal glow, ink brush strokes, mystical atmosphere, elegant pose, soft lighting

#### 场景3：萤火觉醒
```
Chinese ink painting style, golden firefly light blooming from girl's hands, magical particles floating, withered bones glowing with light, dark valley being illuminated, luminous energy spreading, dramatic contrast between light and dark, ink wash technique, cinematic composition
```

#### 场景4：巨灵降临
```
Chinese ink painting style, giant shadow monster emerging from horizon, dark clouds gathering, overwhelming darkness swallowing light, young girl standing defiant, epic scale confrontation, ominous atmosphere, ink wash technique, dramatic lighting, cinematic composition

## 📹 视频生成步骤

### 1. 打开创作页面
- 访问 https://app.pavo-ai.cn/
- 点击「开始创作」或「生成视频」

### 2. 选择模型
- 选择 **Agnes Video 2.5**（高清）或 **Agnes Video 2.5 Flash**（快速）
- 建议使用 Flash 版本提高效率

### 3. 输入提示词
- 复制上面的提示词模板
- 根据需求微调
- 点击「生成」

### 4. 下载视频
- 等待生成完成（通常1-3分钟）
- 下载 MP4 格式视频
- 保存到项目文件夹

### 5. 重复生成
- 为每个场景生成视频片段
- 建议每个片段10-15秒
- 总时长需≥1分钟/集

## 🎬 后期剪辑建议

### 剪辑软件推荐
- 剪映（国内常用）
- Premiere Pro
- Final Cut Pro

### 剪辑流程
1. **导入素材**：将所有生成的视频片段导入
2. **拼接场景**：按剧本顺序排列
3. **添加旁白**：录制或生成配音
4. **添加字幕**：关键剧情字幕
5. **添加音效**：环境音、动作音效
6. **调整节奏**：确保每集≥1分钟
7. **导出成品**：1080p MP4格式

| 剪辑制作 | 9/8（1天）|
| 审核修改 | 9/8-9/9 |
