## 🔧 核心优化点

1. **反派压迫感**：`massive silhouette blocking sky`、`towering above valley` 强调体型差
2. **主角渺小感**：`tiny figure against overwhelming darkness` 制造反差
3. **悬念铺垫**：`suspenseful`、`ominous` 渲染紧张氛围
4. **史诗尺度**：`apocalyptic scale`、`epic confrontation` 提升格局
5. **镜头明确**：`epic wide shot` 控制全景景别
